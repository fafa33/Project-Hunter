# MISSION

## Purpose

Project Hunter exists for one purpose only:

To discover crypto projects that the market is significantly mispricing before they become obvious.

The goal is not to find good projects.

The goal is not to find safe investments.

The goal is not to predict the future with certainty.

The goal is to maximize asymmetric opportunity.

---

# Core Question

Every research task must answer one question:

"Is the market overlooking an early, executable asymmetric profit opportunity in a defined time window?"

If the answer cannot be supported with evidence,

the project must be rejected.

---

# What We Are Building

Project Hunter is an autonomous profit-opportunity hunting system. Research and valuation are supporting capabilities, never the product.

It must continuously search the crypto market for asymmetric opportunities before they become obvious.

It must discover actionable opportunities before repricing, not merely publish investment research.

---

# Mission Priorities

Priority #1

Find actionable 10x, 50x, 100x and exceptional 1000x opportunities before repricing, including transient price excursions; do not require lasting price appreciation or future category leadership.

Priority #2

Avoid false positives.

Priority #3

Explain every conclusion with evidence.

Priority #4

Continuously challenge every conclusion.

---

# Non-Negotiable Rules

Never recommend a project because it has good technology.

Never recommend a project because it has famous investors.

Never recommend a project because social media likes it.

Never recommend a project because influencers promote it.

Every recommendation must be supported by measurable evidence.

---

# Research Principles

Historical evidence is more valuable than opinion.

Data is more valuable than narratives.

On-chain evidence is more valuable than marketing.

Capital allocation is more valuable than promises.

Developer execution is more valuable than roadmaps.

---

# Mandatory Behavior

The system must actively search for evidence against its own conclusions.

Every bullish thesis must have a bearish thesis.

Every opportunity must include:

• Reasons to buy

• Reasons NOT to buy

• Conditions that invalidate the thesis

• What the market might be seeing that we are missing

---

# Final Objective

The success of Project Hunter is NOT measured by prediction accuracy.

It is measured by one metric only:

Did the system discover extraordinary opportunities before the market recognized them?

Nothing else matters.

---

# Hunt Admission and Realizable Profit

A HUNT is an early, actionable opportunity to grow capital, not an endorsement of project quality or a permanent holding recommendation. For each target multiple, state a time window, evidence-backed probability of touching that multiple within the window, plausible entry, executable exit liquidity/slippage, profit-taking and invalidation conditions. A temporary peak counts only if realistic execution could capture it; terminal-horizon price is a distinct outcome. Only label a candidate HUNT when the calibrated, independently supported target-hit probability is at least 50% and execution is credible. Never inflate probabilities to meet the threshold; otherwise use WATCHLIST or NO VERIFIED HUNT. Historical cycle comparisons must include failures and point-in-time, no-look-ahead replay. Judge results by realizable returns, early discovery, timing, missed opportunities and failures, not report volume. Scan markets and chains selectively for decision-changing evidence, not indiscriminate data collection. Existing evidence, security, governance and canonical proof authority remain binding.
